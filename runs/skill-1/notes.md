- Built a hunched, hulking forest warden in a single procedural recipe (glb-creator/tmp/warden.ts): braided bark limbs and torso, fractured stone plates and boulder shoulders, moss patches, ivory claws and fungal shelves, amber emissive eyes, and branching antlers.
- All four prepared PBR sets (stone, bark, moss, ivory) are embedded in monster.glb (12 textures) and applied through the skill's UV generation. The file is about 8.2 MB, roughly 20k triangles, with 0 Khronos errors.
- 18-joint skeleton (Root, Hips, Spine, Chest, Neck, Head, and Shoulder/Elbow/Wrist and Thigh/Knee/Ankle for both sides). Each shape is rigidly bound to one bone.
- A single "attack" clip, 1.2 s: right arm winds up (0.4 s) with a chest and hip counter-twist, slashes through at impact (0.6 s), follows through, then recovers to the exact rest pose at 1.2 s.
- Grounded at Y=0, about 5.2 m tall including antlers (slightly above the 4-5 m target). 3 render passes: initial blockout, a second that fixed the dark and stone-dominated look, tiny claws and hidden eyes, and a third that exposed the head, shrank the back plates, added fungal shelves and lowered the antlers. That is 2 refinements after the first build.
